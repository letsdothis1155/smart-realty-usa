Trust center

How SmartRealty handles money connections

Financial integrations require accurate language. We do not say this product is 100% secure or impossible to hack.

How connections work
When live, payroll switching and deposit accounts will run through regulated partners. The browser talks to SmartRealty; SmartRealty talks to the partner. You should never type an employer password into a SmartRealty form.
What SmartRealty stores
Account identity (name, email, password hash when login is live), waitlist fields, referral codes, goal names and amounts you enter, analytics events, and partner tokens or IDs — not raw payroll passwords.
What SmartRealty does not store
Employer or payroll passwords. We prefer partner tokens over raw account and routing numbers. Marketing analytics strip account numbers, routing numbers, SSNs, and credentials.
Encryption overview
The public site should be served on HTTPS. Passwords, when collected on a live auth host, are hashed with bcrypt. Tokens belong in environment or a secret manager — not in browser config files.
Partner disclosures
SmartRealty is not a bank. FDIC insurance applies only if a specific partner account structure legally supports that statement. We do not claim FDIC insurance on this demo.
Privacy controls
Share cards default to no dollar amounts. Marketing email is opt-in. You can turn off promotional messages at notification preferences.
Account protection
Use a unique password when member login is live. We will never ask for your password by email.
Fraud reporting
If something looks wrong, write ai@smartrealty.us from the address on the account. Do not send full account numbers, SSNs, or payroll passwords in the first message — we will say what is needed.
