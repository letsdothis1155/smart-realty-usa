Last updated: August 19, 2026 · Applies to smartrealty.us and this demo platform

1. Who we are

SMART REALTY.US LLC (“we”, “us”), a Kentucky member-managed LLC filed 3 August 2026 (SOS 1614978.06), operates a demonstration real-estate technology website at smartrealty.us. Trade name: Smart Realty USA. Address: 2611 Harmony Rd, Louisville, KY 40299. Public contact: ai@smartrealty.us. Account requests go to andrewiredale@smartrealty.us.

2. Demo nature

This platform is a product demo. It is not a licensed real-estate brokerage transaction system. Listings, prices, Bitcoin checkout, and rentals are illustrative unless we tell you otherwise in writing.

3. Information we collect

  • Account requests (this host) — full name, email, optional phone, city, state, what you need, and an optional note. No password. GitHub Pages cannot store member logins. Requests email andrewiredale@smartrealty.us and a copy stays on this device.
  • Member login — only if a PHP/API backend is live (not this GitHub Pages host): email and a password hash. We do not store plain-text passwords.
  • Waitlist / contact leads — name, email, interest, optional message, timestamp, optional referral or partner code, and campaign source fields (UTM) if you arrived from a campaign link.
  • Invoices — bill-to name, email, and line item you type on the invoice page stay in the browser until you print. We do not collect cards, routing numbers, or bank account numbers on this site.
  • Technical data — browser type, approximate IP (server logs), cookies/local storage for session and preferences.
  • Usage on your device — saved homes, recent views, demo unlock flags, property goals you create, referral identifiers, notification preferences, and product-analytics events stored in your browser.

4. How we use information

  • Operate sign-in, password reset, and member features
  • Respond to waitlist and concierge requests
  • Improve the demo and fix bugs
  • Send transactional or waitlist-related email when you submit a form

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use hosting providers (e.g. GoDaddy), email delivery on that host, and analytics only if configured. Service providers process data only to run the site.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use browser storage for login sessions, saved homes, compare lists, and demo preferences. You can clear site data in your browser settings.

7. Security

This public host does not store member passwords. If login is later live on a PHP/API host, passwords are hashed (bcrypt). Transport should use HTTPS. No method is 100% secure. Do not send SSNs, EINs, routing numbers, or payroll passwords through these forms.

8. Retention

Account and lead records remain until you request deletion or we remove demo data. Contact us to delete your account or lead entry.

9. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Your choices

  • Request access or deletion: ai@smartrealty.us
  • Unsubscribe from waitlist emails by replying “unsubscribe” or emailing us
  • Stop using the demo and clear browser storage

11. Advertising

Direct sponsor placements are labeled “Sponsored.” We do not load third-party ad network scripts until SMART REALTY.US LLC has an approved publisher account and a publisher ID in server config. When that happens, those networks may set cookies or collect device data under their own policies. You can ask us which networks, if any, are live: ai@smartrealty.us.
