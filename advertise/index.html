Website Advertising Agreement

3 pages / fillable campaign order

Publisher: SMART REALTY.US LLC, Louisville, Kentucky.
Business contact: andrewiredale@smartrealty.us.

The agreement takes effect only after both parties sign a completed campaign order. The downloadable order records your exact placement, dates, creative, fees, reporting, and any amendments. Viewing this page or requesting a quote is not acceptance.

1. Parties and agreement

SMART REALTY.US LLC (Publisher), Louisville, Kentucky, operates smartrealty.us. The Advertiser is the business identified in the completed campaign order. These terms and that order form the agreement only when authorized representatives of both parties sign them. A quote request, website visit, blank template, or payment alone does not create an advertising agreement. The order must identify the parties, exact placement, approved creative, dates, fees, payment deadline, and any agreed reporting. Changes require written approval from both parties. A signed order controls a conflict with these terms only when it expressly identifies the term being changed.

2. Placement and term

Publisher will display the approved advertisement on the exact URL and in the position and format stated in the order. Any rotation, share of display time, mobile treatment, and exclusivity must be stated there. Otherwise the placement is non-exclusive; Publisher may carry other advertisers, including competitors. There is no automatic renewal. Start and end dates and the time zone must be completed. A delayed start requires a revised schedule approved by both parties, not a shortened paid term. No placement outside smartrealty.us is included unless expressly identified.

3. Price and payment

Website placements are publicly offered from $100 per month, subject to placement availability and a written quote. The signed order sets the actual campaign fee, separately stated applicable taxes, total payable in USD, and payment deadline. Fees compensate the display services described in the order, not leads, referrals, closings, or sales. Payment is by the invoice method agreed with Publisher and is due before launch unless the order says otherwise. No credit card or bank details should be sent by ordinary email. No automatic charges or undisclosed fees apply. Creative design or extra revisions cost extra only if separately agreed in writing before the work begins.

4. Creative approval and responsibility

Advertiser will provide its business name, destination URL, ad copy, logo or image if applicable, and any required disclosures by the creative deadline in the order. Advertiser warrants that it has the necessary rights, permissions, licenses, and evidence for its content and claims, and that the linked destination is lawful and safe. Publisher will approve the content and placement in writing before launch and will not materially change the message or destination without Advertiser approval. Publisher may reject misleading, discriminatory, unlawful, infringing, malicious, or unsuitable content. Gambling promotions and deceptive software claims are not accepted.

5. Advertising labels and limited license

Every paid placement will be clearly identified as Advertisement or Paid Advertisement. Placement is not an endorsement, professional recommendation, brokerage service, or sale of customer data. Advertiser grants Publisher a non-exclusive license during the agreed campaign term to host and display the approved creative solely to deliver the order. Publisher may resize or compress it without changing its meaning. Each party keeps ownership of its existing materials. After the campaign, Publisher may retain private records for billing, compliance, and disputes, but public portfolio reuse requires Advertiser approval.

6. Audience and reporting

Publisher does not guarantee impressions, visitors, clicks, leads, search rankings, conversions, revenue, or a particular audience. Published starting rates are not a claim of measured audience value. Before signing, Advertiser may request current traffic evidence; Publisher will provide only available, dated figures with their measurement period and limitations, or state that reliable figures are unavailable. Publisher will provide the live placement URL and a screenshot after launch. Any additional report, metric, measurement source, and frequency must be specified in the order. Unavailable metrics will be identified as unavailable, not estimated or fabricated.

7. Privacy and regulated advertisements

No visitor contact lists, sensitive personal information, or individual leads are included. Third-party scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, behavioral targeting, and data sharing require a separate written arrangement addressing necessary notices, consent, and legal obligations before implementation. Housing and related ads must comply with applicable fair-housing and advertising laws and must not request discriminatory content or targeting. Mortgage, title, brokerage, and other settlement-service advertisements require additional review before acceptance: fees must relate to actual advertising services at a reasonable market value, never an agreement to refer business. A contract label alone does not establish legal compliance. Publisher does not act as a licensed real-estate brokerage or bank under this agreement.

8. Cancellation, interruption, and refunds

Either party may cancel by written notice to the order contact. Before launch, Publisher will refund prepaid placement fees in full; separately authorized creative work already completed remains payable. After launch, cancellation takes effect within two business days of receipt; Publisher will refund the prepaid placement fee for the remaining unused campaign time. If the placement is unavailable, materially incorrect, or removed during paid time, Publisher will notify Advertiser after discovery. Advertiser may choose equivalent replacement time agreed in writing or a proportional refund for affected time. Refunds equal the placement fee multiplied by unused or affected hours divided by total scheduled campaign hours, without double counting, with tax adjustments as applicable. Publisher will issue refunds within ten business days after the amount is determined.

9. Suspension and responsibility for losses

Publisher may suspend an ad immediately for suspected unlawful or harmful content, a compromised destination, or overdue payment, and will notify Advertiser promptly with the reason and any available correction path. If the issue is not resolved, either party may terminate; unused prepaid placement fees are handled under section 8. Amounts for delivered services and separately authorized completed creative work remain payable. Neither party guarantees the other party's business results. Each remains responsible for its own conduct under applicable law. This agreement does not require either party to waive legal rights or accept unlimited indemnity.

10. Notices, disputes, and signatures

Publisher notices go to andrewiredale@smartrealty.us; Advertiser notices go to the business email in the order. A notice known to have failed delivery must be resent through an agreed working contact method. Parties will first try to resolve disputes in good faith. Kentucky law governs, subject to applicable mandatory law; no mandatory arbitration or waiver of access to a court is imposed. If a provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues to the extent lawful. This order and these terms are the entire advertising agreement. Electronic signatures and counterparts may be used where legally valid. Each signer confirms authority to bind the named business. Signing is a separate deliberate act; an inquiry is not acceptance.
