2. Information We Collect

We design our apps to minimize data collection:

  • Daily Cache Wiper (and similar utility apps): Intended to operate primarily on-device. We do not require an account. We do not collect names, emails, photos, contacts, location, or personal files. The AccessibilityService (when used) is limited to clearing cache inside Android Settings after your explicit disclosure and consent; it does not read or transmit your content.
  • Edge Neon: Intended to operate on-device. We do not require an account. With your permission, it uses notification access only to detect that a notification arrived so it can light the screen edges, and display-over-other-apps to draw the border. It does not store, read, or transmit notification content.
  • Other apps (including any music, security, or utility apps): Unless a specific feature requires it and you opt in, we do not collect personal information. Offline-first designs store data only on your device.
  • Automatic / technical data: Device model, OS version, app version, crash reports, and basic anonymous analytics may be collected through Google Play Services or similar platforms solely to improve stability. This data is not personally identifiable.
  • Payments: Subscriptions (e.g., $2/month Premium after trial) are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment card or bank details.
  • Support: If you email us, we receive the information you voluntarily provide.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising networks that track users across apps.
