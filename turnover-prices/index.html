How we rank / no paid placement

Nobody can pay to be listed higher. There is no sponsored rank and no contractor billing. Inside each retailer block, the default order is the lowest total of item price plus shipping. That order is calculated by Smart Realty from the prices in that block. It is not a mix of eBay and Walmart on one card, and it is not a payment from either store.

A Walmart row shows the current price and the note that the price on Walmart.com at the time of purchase governs. eBay rows in a connected feed have to be refreshed at least every 6 hours. The two stores are never combined on one card.

Appliances

Replacement appliances for a rental make-ready. Confirm the opening, the fuel (these examples are electric), and who is allowed to hook them up in Kentucky or Indiana.

Paint and prep

Neutral white and greige, ceiling paint, primer, and the rollers, caulk, and spackle a turnover usually uses. Coverage numbers are rules of thumb, not a bid.

Flooring

LVP and carpet are priced per square foot. The bundle calculator multiplies by a planning quantity for a 1, 2, or 3 bedroom unit. Measure the unit before you order.

Locks and safety

Keypad deadbolts, a Wi-Fi or Z-Wave smart lock, rekey kits, a lockbox, and smoke/CO alarms. Read the smart lock guide before you buy a case.

Turnover basics

Faucets, a toilet with a fill valve, lights, outlets and GFCIs, blinds, HVAC filters, and a leak sensor.
