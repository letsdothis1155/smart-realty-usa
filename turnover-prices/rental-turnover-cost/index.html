Rental turnover cost in Kentuckiana

A make-ready in Louisville or across the river in Jeffersonville, New Albany, or Clarksville usually splits into materials you can price today and labor you still have to bid. This page prices one starter materials list two ways: an eBay subtotal and a Walmart subtotal. They stay apart on purpose.

The starter list is the items checked by default in the bundle calculator: interior paint, a 5-gallon bucket, primer, a roller kit, caulk, spackle, a keypad deadbolt, smoke/CO alarms, GFCI outlets, and HVAC filters. Appliances, flooring, and a smart lock are off until you add them. Bedroom counts change the quantity, using planning numbers, not a tape measure of your unit.

What the subtotal leaves out

Labor is the larger unknown: painters in Jeffersonville, a flooring installer in New Albany, a locksmith rekey in Louisville, an appliance install in Clarksville. The materials subtotal is not that invoice. Trash-out, carpet stretch, drywall beyond spackle, and a water heater swap are separate jobs. Ask for those on the quote form.

Shipping is inside each retailer total when the example offer charges it. A “free shipping” row can still lose to a lower item price that charges freight, which is why each block sorts by item plus shipping.
