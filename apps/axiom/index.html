Axiom

Android · com.iredale.axiom

Axiom is a Casio-style scientific calculator. Degrees, radians, and grads. Trig, logs, memory, and on-device evaluation. It does not use AccessibilityService and it does not scan other apps.

How it makes money

  • Free to install on Google Play
  • Optional Axiom Pro: no upgrade banner and on-device history
  • Monthly, yearly, or lifetime unlock through Google Play Billing
  • U.S. price is set in Play Console (confirm there before quoting it)
