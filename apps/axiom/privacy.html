Privacy Policy

Axiom · Package com.iredale.axiom · Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Axiom (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android application published by SMART REALTY.US LLC (Andrew Iredale Jr.).

1. Who we are

Axiom is a scientific calculator for Android. Contact: andrewiredalejr@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account.

  • Calculations: Expressions, graph settings, and related values stay on your device. They are not uploaded.

  • No sale of personal data.

3. Permissions

The current Play edition does not use AccessibilityService. It does not scan other apps. It does not request storage or All-files access. It does not collect your calculations off the device.

4. How we use information

  • To evaluate the expressions you enter on the device
  • To draw graphs and conversions you request on the device

5. Data sharing

We do not share your calculations with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate for an on-device calculator. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices
