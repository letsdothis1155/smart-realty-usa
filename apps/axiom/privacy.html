Privacy Policy

Axiom · Package com.iredale.axiom · Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Axiom (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android application published by SMART REALTY.US LLC (Andrew Iredale Jr.).

1. Who we are

Axiom is a scientific calculator for Android. Contact: andrewiredalejr@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account.

  • Calculations and history: Expressions and history stay on your device. They are not uploaded.
  • Subscription / purchase status: Handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details. We only receive entitlement status needed to unlock Axiom Pro.
  • No advertising ID tracking and no third-party ad SDKs in the current app.
  • No sale of personal data.

3. Permissions

The Play edition uses the Internet permission only so Google Play Billing can confirm purchases. The App does not use AccessibilityService. The App does not scan other apps. The App does not request storage or All-files access.

4. How we use information

  • To evaluate the expressions you enter on the device
  • To store optional history on the device when Axiom Pro is unlocked
  • To restore purchases through Google Play

5. Data sharing

Payment and subscription management are provided by Google Play. We do not share your calculations with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data retention

Local preferences and history remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Google retains purchase records according to Google’s policies.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate for an on-device calculator. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices

  • Cancel an Axiom Pro subscription anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

11. Contact
