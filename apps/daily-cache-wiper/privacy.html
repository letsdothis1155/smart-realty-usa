Privacy Policy

Daily Cache Wiper · Package com.dailycachewiper.app · Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Daily Cache Wiper (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android application published by SMART REALTY.US LLC (Andrew Iredale Jr.).

1. Who we are

Daily Cache Wiper is a mobile utility that guides a cache-only clean of one app you choose. Contact: andrewiredalejr@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account.

  • Subscription / purchase status: Handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details. We only receive subscription entitlement status needed to unlock Premium features.
  • Local usage counters: Free-tier daily clean counts and lifetime clean counts are stored only on your device.
  • No advertising ID tracking and no third-party ad SDKs in the current app.
  • No sale of personal data.

3. AccessibilityService API

The App uses the Android AccessibilityService API. It is not an accessibility tool. After an in-app disclosure and your consent, the service may read on-screen labels only inside the Android Settings app so it can tap “Storage & cache” and “Clear cache” for the one app you selected. That access is armed only for a short session after you tap Clean. The Settings text is not collected, stored, transmitted, shared, or sold. You can disable the service in Android Settings → Accessibility.

4. Storage permission

The current App does not request storage or “All files access.” App names and icons used in the picker stay in memory on your device so you can choose a target. They are not stored, transmitted, shared, or sold. The App does not upload your photos, documents, or personal files.

5. How we use information

  • To perform the guided cache-only clean you request
  • To enforce free vs Premium limits
  • To restore purchases through Google Play

6. Data sharing

Payment and subscription management are provided by Google Play. We do not share AccessibilityService readings or device files with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Google retains purchase records according to Google’s policies.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate for an on-device utility. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

10. Your choices

  • Disable Daily Cache Wiper in Android Settings → Accessibility
  • Cancel Premium anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

12. Contact
