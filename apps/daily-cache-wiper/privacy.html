Privacy Policy

Daily Cache Wiper · Package com.dailycachewiper.app · Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Daily Cache Wiper (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android application published by Andrew Iredale / Smart Realty USA.

1. Who we are

Daily Cache Wiper is a mobile utility that helps you scan and delete cache and temporary files you choose to clean. Contact: andrewiredalejr@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account.

  • Subscription / purchase status: Handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details. We only receive subscription entitlement status needed to unlock Premium features.
  • Local usage counters: Free-tier daily wipe counts are stored only on your device.
  • No advertising ID tracking and no third-party ad SDKs in the current app.
  • No sale of personal data.

3. Storage permission

The App may request storage or “All files access” permission solely to scan and delete cache-like and temporary files on your device at your request. The App does not upload your photos, documents, or personal files to our servers.

4. How we use information

  • To provide cache scanning and wiping features
  • To enforce free vs Premium limits
  • To restore purchases through Google Play

5. Data sharing

Payment and subscription management are provided by Google Play. We do not share your device files with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Google retains purchase records according to Google’s policies.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate for an on-device utility. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices

  • Revoke storage permission in Android Settings
  • Cancel Premium anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

11. Contact

Questions: andrewiredalejr@gmail.com
