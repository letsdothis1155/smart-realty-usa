## Step-by-step

### 1) Check the name is available (~2 min)

Search Kentucky business entities:

**https://sosbes.sos.ky.gov/BusSearchNProfile/search.aspx**

Search: `Smart Realty` and `Smart Realty USA`

- If **exact** name is taken, try:
  - `Smart Realty USA LLC`
  - `Smart Realty USA Group LLC`
  - `SRU Holdings LLC` (then DBA “Smart Realty USA” later)

Name must be distinguishable and usually include **LLC** (or Limited Liability Company).

### 2) Choose registered agent

Kentucky requires a registered agent with a **Kentucky street address**.

- **Cheapest:** You (Andrew Iredale) if you have a reliable KY address and can receive daytime mail  
- **Privacy / convenience:** Paid registered agent service  

### 3) File Articles of Organization — $40

**Online (recommended):** Kentucky One Stop / business portal  

- Portal: **https://onestop.ky.gov/**  
- Start / business filings: often via **https://onestop.portal.ky.gov/onestopportal/** or links from **https://sos.ky.gov/**

Create an account → file **Articles of Organization** for a **domestic LLC** → pay **$40**.

**What you’ll enter (typical fields):**

- LLC name: `Smart Realty USA LLC`
- Principal office address (KY street)
- Registered agent name + KY street address
- Organizer: Andrew Iredale
- Management: member-managed is simplest if it’s only you
- Effective date: upon filing (usual)

**Mail / SOS info:**  
Secretary of State business filings — https://sos.ky.gov/bus/business-filings/Pages/default.aspx  
Phone often listed: **(502) 564-3490** (confirm on site)

Keep the **approval email / PDF** and any **organization number** the state assigns.

### 4) Get a free EIN (same day online)

**https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online**

- Responsible party: **Andrew Iredale**
- Entity: LLC  
- Reason: started a new business  
- Save the CP 575 confirmation PDF  

### 5) Open a business bank account

Bring:

- Articles of Organization (approved)
- EIN letter
- Your ID
- Operating agreement (simple one-member is fine — template from attorney or reputable source)

### 6) Free D‑U‑N‑S Number

After LLC + address are set:

1. Lookup: https://www.dnb.com/en-us/smb/duns/duns-lookup.html  
2. Apply: https://www.dnb.com/en-us/smb/duns/get-a-duns.html → **U.S.-based business**  
3. Legal name: **exact** `Smart Realty USA LLC`  
4. Owner: **Andrew Iredale**  
5. Skip paid packages unless you want them
