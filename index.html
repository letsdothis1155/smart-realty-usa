Owner setup · not a customer form

Get a free D‑U‑N‑S® Number

A D‑U‑N‑S Number is a free 9-digit business ID from Dun & Bradstreet. It is not a real-estate brokerage license. Use the filed Kentucky name exactly. Do not file a second LLC.

Copy these fields into D&B

Apply (free path)

  1. Lookup first — you may already have one.
  2. If not, request a free D‑U‑N‑S on Dun & Bradstreet.
  3. Choose U.S.-based business.
  4. Decline paid “credit builder” packages unless you want them — the number itself is free.
  5. Wait for email confirmation (often days; can take up to ~30 days).
  6. Store the 9-digit number in your password manager + domain-config.js.
