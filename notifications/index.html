What we may send — and what we will not

Transactional notices stay separate from marketing. We do not send account numbers, routing numbers, or payroll credentials by email or push. Push is prepared here; it is not blasting your lock screen.

Frequency caps apply. We will not use countdown-scarcity spam. Examples: “Your direct deposit setup is ready for the next step.” “You made progress toward your Home Fund.” “Your payroll connection needs attention.”
