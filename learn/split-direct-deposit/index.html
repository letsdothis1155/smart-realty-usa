Split direct deposit

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

A split deposit tells payroll to send part of net pay to one destination and the remainder to another. Common patterns: a fixed dollar amount to rent or savings, with leftover to checking; or a percentage split.

Limits you may hit

  • Some employers allow only two destinations. Others allow more.
  • A fixed dollar split can fail or send $0 to the second account if net pay is smaller than the fixed amount that week (after taxes, garnishments, or a short paycheck).
  • Percentages scale with pay. Fixed dollars do not.

SmartSplit vs payroll split

Payroll split happens at the employer. SmartSplit is a SmartRealty idea for organizing money after it arrives. SmartSplit is labeled Coming Soon and is not operational. The on-site visualizer is illustrative only and does not move funds.

Splitting pay is an organization tool. It does not guarantee you will reach a down payment or keep a rental. Check your employer’s form for the rules that actually apply to you.
