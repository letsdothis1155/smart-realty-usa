Moving budget

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

Moves have a long tail. The truck is obvious. The week of takeout, extra laundry, and overlapping rent is what blows a thin plan.

Line items to price yourself

  • Labor: DIY friends, or a licensed mover you actually hired
  • Vehicle or truck, fuel, tolls, parking
  • Boxes, tape, and something to protect a mattress
  • Overlap: last week at the old place plus first week at the new one
  • Address changes that cost money (driver’s license, parking permits)
  • Time off work, if the move is not on a weekend

Get written quotes from movers if you use them. This page is not a quote and SmartRealty is not a moving company. A Moving Fund goal is a place to park the number you choose.
