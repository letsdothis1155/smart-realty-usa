Saving for a down payment

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational · not lending advice

A down payment is the part of a home’s purchase price that is not borrowed. The percentage a lender will accept depends on the loan type, your credit file, the property, and rules that change. There is no single “correct” percent that SmartRealty can publish as a promise.

How people use a named fund

Giving the money a name — Down Payment, First Home — makes it less likely to blend into everyday checking. That is organization. It is not pre-approval, and it is not a locked rate.

What to pair with the number

  • Closing costs are separate. See the companion guide.
  • Emergency cash still matters. Emptying every reserve for a down payment can leave you fragile after closing.
  • Talk to a licensed lender for actual program rules. SmartRealty is not a lender and not a bank.

You can track a target on the goals page. Progress there is what you record. Reaching a number does not guarantee a purchase.
