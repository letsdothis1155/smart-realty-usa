Saving for closing costs

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

Closing costs are the fees and prepaid items that show up around the day you close a purchase or refinance. They are not the down payment. People who only save for the down payment are often surprised here.

Categories you will usually see

  • Lender charges and third-party reports (appraisal, credit report).
  • Title, escrow, or settlement fees.
  • Prepaid taxes and insurance, if the loan uses an escrow account.
  • Recording fees set by the locality.

The dollar amount depends on the contract, the loan, and the place. A Loan Estimate from a licensed lender is the document that itemizes a specific deal. This page is not that document.

Create a Closing Costs goal if you want a separate bucket. SmartRealty does not quote closing costs and does not guarantee a purchase will close.
