Property maintenance budget

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

After closing, the house still spends money. A maintenance reserve is cash you keep for repairs and replacements so a broken water heater is not a credit-card event. Rules of thumb you see online are guesses. Your roof age, HVAC, and local taxes are the real inputs.

Buckets owners actually use

  • Repairs and small jobs
  • Insurance deductibles
  • Property taxes if they are not escrowed
  • HOA or condo dues, if any
  • Planned replacements (roof, HVAC, appliances)

SmartRealty can hold a Property Maintenance or Emergency Housing Fund goal. That is a reminder, not an insurance policy and not an investment return. We do not claim a percentage of home value is “the” right reserve.
