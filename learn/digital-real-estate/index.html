Digital real estate

What SmartRealty means by digital real estate

Updated 19 August 2026 · Company definition

On this site, digital real estate means the software layer around physical housing: organizing pay, naming goals, keeping records, and — later — connecting people to licensed help they ask for. It is not a deed. Using the app does not make you an owner of land, a unit, or a security.

The story we are building

Get paid → organize → build a goal → prepare for property. The financial tools are how people arrive. The property goals are why they stay. Progress is visible so there is a reason to return. Invites should come from something that helped, not from spam.

What it is not

  • Not a token offering or a claim that a coin is a house
  • Not a licensed brokerage transaction system on this demo
  • Not a bank, and not FDIC insured as SmartRealty
  • Not a guarantee of approval, savings, or ownership

The company also runs a public real-estate demo (listings, Blue Book, Bitcoin checkout quotes) and a Bitcoin node explorer. Those are separate surfaces. Digital real estate, as a phrase here, is the housing-money workspace described on direct deposit and goals.
