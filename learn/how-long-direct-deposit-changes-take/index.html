How long direct deposit changes take

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

There is no single nationwide timer. Timing depends on when payroll builds its ACH file, whether your employer runs weekly or biweekly, and whether the new account needs a prenote or micro-deposit check.

A realistic expectation

If you submit a change after the current cycle’s cutoff, the next payday often still goes to the old destination. Many people should plan on one full payroll cycle, sometimes two if the bank rejects the first file. Same-day ACH exists, but your employer has to use it; you cannot assume they will.

What to watch

  • The confirmation screen or email from payroll — not a text from an unknown number.
  • The first posted credit on the new account. “Pending” is not the same as posted.
  • A returned ACH if the routing or account number was mistyped. That can add days.

SmartRealty will not promise instant switching. Any live product we ship later should show status as pending until the partner and the payer confirm. Keep the old account funded for bills until you see the new path work once.
