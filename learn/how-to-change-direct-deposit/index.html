How to change direct deposit

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

You change direct deposit with the organization that already pays you. That is usually HR, a payroll portal (ADP, Paychex, Gusto, Workday, and others), or a gig app’s tax/payout screen. It is not something a website can finish by guessing your employer password.

A practical sequence

  1. Open the new account details from the bank or partner that holds the destination. Confirm the name matches the name payroll uses.
  2. Sign in to the official payroll or gig payout page. Bookmark that URL from the company’s own site, not from a search ad.
  3. Add the new destination. If the system allows a split, decide entire net pay vs a percentage vs a fixed dollar amount.
  4. Leave the old account open until you see at least one payday land where you intended.
  5. Save a screenshot or confirmation number. Payroll cutoffs are real; a change after the file is built often waits until the following cycle.

What not to do

Do not email full account and routing numbers to an address you cannot verify. Do not give anyone your payroll password. SmartRealty will not ask for that password. If a partner switch is ever offered here, the partner’s own connection flow should handle credentials.

Changing deposit instructions does not guarantee you will save more, qualify for a mortgage, or buy a home. It only changes where eligible pay is sent.
