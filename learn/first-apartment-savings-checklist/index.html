First apartment savings checklist

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

The rent on the listing is rarely the cash you need the week you get keys. Landlords and local law differ, so treat this as a list of questions, not a quote.

  • First month’s rent
  • Security deposit (sometimes last month’s rent as well — ask, do not assume)
  • Application, credit, or admin fees
  • Utility deposits or connection fees
  • Renter’s insurance, if the lease requires it
  • A lock, a cheap tool kit, and something to sleep on
  • Moving labor or a truck

Add the lines you know. Leave a buffer for the ones you do not. SmartRealty can hold those as separate goals (Rent, Security Deposit, Furniture, Moving Fund). Reaching a number does not guarantee a landlord will approve an application.
