Direct deposit for gig workers

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

If you drive, deliver, freelance, or sell through apps, you may have three or four payout destinations already. Each platform is its own payer. Changing one does not change the others.

What to ask each app

  • Where is the official payout or tax screen? Use the in-app menu, not a link from a text.
  • Is instant cash-out a card rail with a fee, or an ACH deposit?
  • Can you split payouts, or is it one destination only?
  • How many days after a job does the standard deposit post?

Uneven pay

Gig income is lumpy. A housing goal still helps as a named bucket — rent, a moving fund, a maintenance reserve — but the amount you can send will change week to week. Do not set a fixed transfer that assumes last month’s peak.

SmartRealty’s preview can remember more than one payer path later. It cannot make gig income steady, and it is not a bank. Instant cash-out products from platforms are their products, with their fees and risks.
