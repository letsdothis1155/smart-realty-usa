Payroll direct deposit setup

Updated 19 August 2026 · Educational

Before you hand anyone a form or click submit in a payroll portal, walk this list. It is written for a human, not as a promise that SmartRealty will file the form for you.

  1. Confirm the destination account is open and can receive ACH credits.
  2. Copy the routing number from the bank or partner, not from memory. Routing numbers are nine digits.
  3. Copy the account number carefully. Transposed digits are a common reason a first file returns.
  4. Mark checking or savings as the bank classifies it.
  5. Match the account title to the name payroll uses. A nickname on a debit card is not the legal name.
  6. Ask HR or the portal for the cutoff date for the next payday.
  7. Keep the previous account open until the new destination posts once.
  8. Never put a payroll password in email or in a SmartRealty form.

When SmartRealty offers partner-assisted switching, this same checklist still applies. The partner is the regulated connection. SmartRealty is not a bank and is not your employer.
